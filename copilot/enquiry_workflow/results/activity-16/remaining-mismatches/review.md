3. Test trusted case-status input and short follow-up confirmations. Existing customers should route to Support and new customers to Sales for the approved enquiry types. Confirm both deferred replies suppress repeated questions.
4. Report accuracy and provisional coverage separately, including performance on explicit-status versus unknown-status cases. Continue reporting the original exact-owner metric.
